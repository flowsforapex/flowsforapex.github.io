Once workflows are running in production, you need to understand their health, track performance, and maintain compliance audit trails. Flows for APEX provides built-in dashboards, reporting, and operational monitoring capabilities.

## What It Is

Monitoring, Audit & Operations covers the dashboard, reporting, and operational analytics capabilities of Flows for APEX. This includes built-in dashboards showing process and task statistics, performance metrics, audit trails for debugging and compliance, and the ability to generate archived summaries of historical instances.

## How It Works in Flows for APEX

Flows for APEX includes:

- **Built-in Dashboard**: Real-time visibility into process and task statistics, including processing times, waiting times, and completion rates.
- **Detailed Reports**: Generate detailed reports on workflow timeline, performance, and efficiency across all instances.
- **Audit Trails**: Complete audit trail for compliance and accountability—track who touched each process, what decisions were made, and when.
- **Instance Summary Archives**: Automated creation of Instance Summary archive documents for historical reference and compliance retention.
- **Performance Analytics**: Understand bottlenecks, wait times, and process efficiency metrics.

All operational data is retained in the database alongside your application data—no external analytics systems required.

## Business Capabilities Provided

- Real-time visibility into workflow performance and SLA compliance
- Detailed historical records for compliance auditing and process improvement
- Performance analytics to identify bottlenecks and optimize processes
- Automated archival of process summaries for retention requirements
- Clear operational metrics to support process management decisions
- Support for regulatory and compliance requirements around process tracking

## Why Flows for APEX

Flows for APEX keeps operational data in the database alongside your application data—no integration with external analytics systems needed. Because workflow and application data share the same transaction context, your reports and dashboards reflect a consistent, point-in-time view of the system.

Custom workflow solutions often scatter audit data across application logs and database tables, making compliance reporting difficult. Flows for APEX centralizes workflow auditing and performance data so reporting is straightforward and reliable.

External workflow engines require separate analytics systems and network calls. Flows for APEX operational data is always accessible, always consistent, and always audit-ready.

## Best-Fit Use Cases

- Regulated or mission-critical applications requiring compliance audit trails
- Operations teams that need real-time visibility into workflow health
- Organizations implementing process management disciplines
- Applications where SLA compliance and performance tracking are required
- Business users needing operational dashboards and reporting

## Edition Notes

Core monitoring, auditing, and operational dashboards are available in both editions of Flows for APEX. Additional performance monitoring features are on the development agenda for future releases of the Enterprise Edition.
